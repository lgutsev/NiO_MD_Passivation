# ITO / SAM hole-selective contact literature matrix

- Date: 2026-09-26
- Method: WebSearch + WebFetch on publisher pages and DOI resolvers; Europe PMC full-text XML for OA papers (methods paragraphs parsed locally); institutional repository / OSTI / arXiv author manuscripts converted with pdftotext; Crossref, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar for DOI metadata and abstracts; COD CIF for crystallography. Wiley, ACS, RSC, Science and IUCr landing pages mostly returned 403, so those papers are A unless an author manuscript was found. Details are paraphrased; no quotation longer than a few words.
- Verification legend: **V** = DOI and the cited details seen at source (full text, AAM or SI); **A** = abstract / metadata only (details beyond the abstract not confirmed); **U** = unverified (what is missing is stated).
- Stack classes: `ITO/SAM` (SAM directly on ITO), `ITO/NiOx/SAM` (NiOx interlayer), `other` (FTO, sapphire, Si/ITO recombination layer, model oxide, etc.). Never collapsed; a paper with more than one stack gets each label with the condition that applies.
- Molar masses used for ratio conversions (computed from formulas, not from papers): MeO-2PACz C16H18NO5P 335.3; Me-4PACz C18H22NO3P 331.3; 2PACz C14H14NO3P 275.2; BPPA C7H9O3P 172.1 g/mol. For MeO-2PACz/Me-4PACz, mass, molar and equal-mg/ml volume ratios agree to ~1%.

What a nonreactive, fixed-substrate force field of this kind cannot establish:
- Binding mode (mono/bi/tridentate), P-O-In/Sn condensation, deprotonation, or water release; "anchoring" is an LJ/Coulomb contact.
- Adsorption energies comparable to DFT or experiment (E_ads mixes UFF LJ, fixed charges and a bare, rigid, unhydroxylated oxide).
- Equilibrium packing or density: 1 ns is short relative to minutes of solution contact plus 100 C anneal; a plateau in mesh area is not equilibrium; no EtOH, so micelle break-up (the DLS observations) is not modeled; molecule number is an input, not an outcome.
- Sn, OH groups, adventitious carbon or UV-O3/plasma state, so pretreatment dependence (A4) and Sn surface enrichment (C8) are absent.
- Ratio fidelity: B3-1 simulates a premixed system although its best device is sequential, and its 9:1 is molar in MD vs w/w in experiment.
- Work function or dipole (no polarization/electronic structure); those numbers come from DFT/UPS.

Claims the authors did draw from it (B3-1): PyCA-3F suppresses 2PACz aggregation and increases coverage; a H-bonded PyCA-3F/2PACz complex restricts 2PACz mobility; PyCA-3F acts as a transient surface "guide". These are qualitative and rest on nonbonded contacts only.

## Implications for our model

1. Pilot ratio: **MeO-2PACz:Me-4PACz 1:1 molar** (equal to 1:1 mass within 1%). Basis: 1:1 is the most frequently used ratio in verified methods (B2-4 w/w, B2-5 v/v), it gives the best statistics for detecting demixing, and the molar/mass ambiguity is negligible for this pair. Run **1:2 molar** next because it is the only stated ratio verified for a direct `ITO/SAM` stack (B2-2); 1:3 (B2-3) is `ITO/NiOx/SAM` and is a lower priority.
2. The solution ratio need not equal the surface ratio (competitive adsorption, B4-3), so the pilot should report the adsorbed composition rather than assume it. No experimental surface composition exists for this mixture (B2 gap).
3. Target densities: 2PACz 2.31+/-0.38 molecules/nm2 (XRR, A3; stated similar on ITO), steric ceiling ~2.3 nm-2, I-2PACz 1.61, tBu-2PACz 1.33. No measured value for MeO-2PACz or Me-4PACz was found; given their larger 3,6-substituents, expect <= 2PACz, roughly 1.4-2.3 nm-2. A scan at 1.5 / 1.9 / 2.3 nm-2 brackets this; 1.6 nm-2 is what B4-3 loaded (an input, not a measurement). Per 1.773 nm2 (111) cell that is ~2.7-4.1 molecules.
4. Orientation check: carbazole plane at 61-65 deg to the surface (A3, NEXAFS, all X-2PACz on ITO); layer ~0.9-1.0 nm (XRR). These are the best experimental observables for validating tilt and thickness.
5. Binding mode: experiment favors deprotonated, bound PA with <1 free P-OH per molecule (A3) and predominantly bidentate on ITO (C11); site-limited density 2.2 (tridentate) to 4.4 (bidentate) nm-2 on alumina. A nonreactive MD cannot choose; impose the anchor state explicitly (e.g. bidentate phosphonate with charges balancing removed OH/H) and state it as an assumption.
6. Hydroxyl density: keep 3 dissociated H2O per (111) 1x1 cell = 6 OH/cell = 3.38 OH/nm2, placed as O_wH on the specific In(5c) pairs plus O_sH on the adjacent O(3c) around the In(6c) region (C3/C4), not spread uniformly. Document which OH are counted. Add a higher-OH sensitivity case for plasma/UV-O3 ITO (C13).
7. Termination and structure: bulk-like, nonpolar In2O3(111)-(1x1) (C3-C5). Build from the COD 2310009 (Marezio) coordinates, or switch to Hagleitner 2012 (a = 10.1150 A) and cite it; the present O set matches neither source (C1) and should be replaced or re-sourced, although the <0.04 A difference is negligible after relaxation.
8. Potential: Walsh et al. 2009 In2O3 pair potential (C7), with Buckingham to LJ refit plus geometric mixing as in B4-3, is the only documented In2O3 recipe used for SAM MD in this literature; B3-1 is not a usable citation for In2O3 parameters. No ReaxFF In-Sn-O was found (C15).
9. Sn: omitting Sn is what B3-1 and B4-3 did; if included, ~10% Sn with O_i clusters (C8) and possible surface enrichment (C8, C13) matter for anchor sites. Record it as an explicit approximation.
10. Simulation length: B3-1 used 1 ns; B3-2 used 1 + 10 ns. Aggregation claims need at least the B3-2 length plus replicas; include EtOH if micelle break-up is a question (DLS: 2PACz ~70 nm in EtOH, Me-4PACz ~3 nm in DMF/MeOH).
